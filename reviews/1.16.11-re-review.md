## Blockers

- [ ] **(blocker, verified, correctness + blast-radius) `HeadingIds.next` never terminates when a page has two headings whose slug is `example`.** `src/doc-system/heading-id.ts:47-54`.
  - **Cause:** 0d9043a83 changed the loop guard from `this.seen.has(id)` to `taken(id)`, which also rejects anything matching `/^example-\d+$/`. On the second `next('example')` every candidate is `example-N`, so `while (taken(id))` has no exit.
  - **Reproduced by both verifiers:** `new HeadingIds().next('example')` twice prints `example` and is then killed by SIGALRM. `renderDocMarkdown('# T\n\n## Example\n\na\n\n## Example\n\nb\n')` never returns. Controls terminate (`usage` x2, `examples` x2, `example-2` -> `example-2-1`).
  - **Reach:** `HeadingIds` is used by `src/doc-system/render.ts` (the one renderer, build and client) and by `src/doc-system/site/single-source.ts:180` (inset anchors). An adopter page with `## Example` under each of two components hangs `buildSite` and the dev server rebuild with no message, and freezes the tab on a client-side render. Any level, any case, any punctuation that slugs to `example`.
  - **Fix:** let the suffix loop leave the reserved space. Either suffix the reserved candidate itself (`example` -> `example-1-1`) or give the `example` base a suffix form outside `example-\d+`.
  - **Clears with:** unit tests in `src/doc-system/heading-id.test.ts` for `['example','example']`, `['example','example','example']` and `'Example'` followed by `'Example 1'`, plus one `renderDocMarkdown` case with two `## Example` headings. Each must hang or fail with the fix reverted, so give them a timeout.
  - **Re-review scope:** correctness + blast-radius over the remediation diff only (`heading-id.ts` and its two callers).
