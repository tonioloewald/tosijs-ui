## Nits (verified)
- **Peer-floor raise in a patch (blast-radius; confirmed, severity uncertain, lowered to nit).** `package.json:70` raises the tosijs peer from ^1.10.3 to ^1.10.9 in a patch release.
  - **Who is affected:** an adopter who pins tosijs exactly between 1.10.3 and 1.10.8 gets an ERESOLVE error, or tosijs is upgraded as a side effect.
  - **Silent failure:** an adopter who stays below 1.10.9 (via `--legacy-peer-deps` or bun's lenient peer handling) gets toggled rows that jump, with nothing at runtime to say why.
  - **The loop was nearly free:** on 1.10.9 the removed loop exited on its first check and wrote nothing.
  - **Mitigating factors:**
    - 1.10.9 is inside the caret range most adopters already resolve.
    - The same release fixed virtual lists (#3302), so adopters want it anyway.
    - CLAUDE.md says the floor "encodes specific upstream fixes, not a date".
    - The CHANGELOG `[change]` bullet states the requirement.
  - **Recommendation:** accept as written, and have the maintainer rule on it. The alternative is to keep the loop one more cycle and leave the floor at ^1.10.3.
