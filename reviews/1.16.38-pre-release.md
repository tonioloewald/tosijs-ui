## Provenance

The first cut (235057f4e, `embedLocalImages`, root-absolute srcs) was written by the downstream
project's agent before it had adopted the coding practices; the owner kept it and handed review
and release to this repo. Its commit has no blank line after the subject, so its `[fix]` bullet
is part of the subject and `release-check` cannot see it (2 annotations counted, 3 written). The
CHANGELOG covers it. Added here: relative srcs, the `strict` failure, docs, then the review fixes
below.

## Is it a patch?

Additive for every site that was not already shipping a broken book. The one new failure, an
unbindable image under `strict`, was put to the maintainer: **fail the build**, including an image
only the deployed host serves, on the 1.16.15 ground (the book is the broken artifact). Recorded
in CLAUDE.md.
